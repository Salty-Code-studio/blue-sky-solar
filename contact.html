Questions we hear a lot.

What does a system cost?

It depends on how much power you use and what you want to fix. We give a free consultation and a custom quote. Material prices move with purchase and transport costs, so ask for the current figure.

How fast does it pay back?

Hybrid and off-grid systems usually pay back in 4 to 5 years. Grid tied is the fastest, at around 2 years.

Will my roof work?

Most likely. We install on sloped, flat, tile and corrugated sheet roofs. No good roof? We can build a ground frame. One customer has 32 panels on a frame at the back of the property and 17 on the garage and pool shade roof.

How will the panels sit on my roof?

We fly a drone over your roof. The photo becomes a design drawing that shows the panel layout. It comes with your quote, so you see it before you sign.

Does it survive salt and heat?

Bonaire gets over 3,000 sunshine hours a year, with high heat and salt air. Every system we build has to handle both.

How much maintenance is there?

Clean the panels about twice a year with water only. No cleaning agents.

I live abroad part of the year.

We set up the monitoring app with you before we leave. We also offer remote monitoring and service for vacation homes.

Can I buy single parts?

Yes. We sell individual materials, even a single panel. See what we keep in stock.

Can I visit the office?

Yes, by appointment. We are often out on roofs, so message first. The coffee is always fresh.

How fast do you answer?

Within one business day. WhatsApp is usually quickest.
