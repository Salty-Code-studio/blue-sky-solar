Questions about EV charging.

Where can you install an EV charger?

At home, at your business or at public locations.

Is the charger powered by solar?

Yes. Our EV charging is powered by solar.

Can I offer charging to guests or customers?

Yes. A charger at your business or for your guests is something we install.

Who installs it?

Our own crew, not subcontractors. We also set up the monitoring app with you before we leave.

Do I need new panels?

Tell us what you have in your free consultation. We size the solar side around what you want to fix.

What does EV charging cost?

It depends on your setup and your goal. We give a free consultation and a custom quote. Material prices move with purchase and transport costs, so ask for the current figure.
