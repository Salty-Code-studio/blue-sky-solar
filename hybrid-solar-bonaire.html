Sun by day. Battery at night. Grid as the safety net.

A hybrid system is solar and a battery working with the grid. Here is what happens through the day.

Panels feed the house first

Your panels power the home while the sun is up. What you do not use goes into the battery.

The battery holds the rest

When the sun goes down, the battery takes over. You draw less from WEB.

The grid is still there

If the battery runs low, the house pulls from WEB. If WEB drops out, you keep your power.

Who a hybrid fits.

  • You want a lower bill and power that survives an outage.
  • WEB cuts and spikes have cost you comfort or damaged equipment.
  • You already have panels and want to add a battery. Louis and Irene did exactly that, adding a battery to their ten panels.
  • You want to keep the grid as a safety net instead of leaving it.

Questions about hybrid solar.

Does hybrid solar keep my power on when WEB goes down?

Yes. A hybrid system has a battery, so you have power during outages.

How long does a hybrid take to pay back?

Usually 4 to 5 years. Grid tied pays back faster, at around 2 years, but it gives no backup in an outage.

Can I add a battery to the panels I already have?

Often yes. Louis and Irene had ten panels and added a battery for blackouts. Tell us what you have and we will look at it.

Will I still pay WEB anything?

The grid stays connected. Louis and Irene now pay only the fixed monthly fee.

How do I see what the system is doing?

We set up the monitoring app with you before we leave. Battery level, solar output and grid use show on your phone.

What does a hybrid system cost?

It depends on how much power you use and what you want to fix. We give a free consultation and a custom quote. Material prices move with purchase and transport costs, so ask for the current figure.
