Who off-grid fits.

  • Your site is remote and the grid is far away or not there.
  • You want full independence from WEB, its bill and its outages.
  • You are ready to size the system to your real use. We size the panels and batteries around how much you use each day.
  • You have space for a battery room. Mario on our crew builds them.

Questions about off-grid solar.

Can a whole home run off-grid on Bonaire?

Yes. Off-grid means fully independent of the grid. The batteries power everything.

How long does off-grid take to pay back?

Usually 4 to 5 years, the same range as a hybrid system.

What if my roof is not suitable?

We can build a ground frame. One customer built a frame at the back of the property for 32 panels and put 17 more on the garage and pool shade roof.

How does it handle bad weather?

One customer wrote that his fully off-grid system performed exceptionally well under all weather conditions after two months.

Can you watch an off-grid system for me?

We set up the monitoring app with you before we leave. We also offer remote monitoring and service for remote sites and vacation homes.

What does an off-grid system cost?

It depends on how much power you use and what you want to fix. We give a free consultation and a custom quote. Material prices move with purchase and transport costs, so ask for the current figure.
