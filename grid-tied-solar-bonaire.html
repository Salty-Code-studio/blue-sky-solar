Questions about grid tied solar.

Does grid tied solar work in a blackout?

No. A grid tied system gives no backup during outages. If you want power in an outage, choose hybrid.

How long does grid tied take to pay back?

Around 2 years. It is the fastest payback of our systems.

Why choose grid tied over hybrid?

Grid tied is the lowest cost way to cut the bill. Hybrid adds a battery and outage power, and pays back in about 4 to 5 years.

Do I need a battery?

No. Solar works during the day and the grid supplies the rest.

How long does the install take?

Typically two days. One customer's two-house property was done in a day and a half.

What does a grid tied system cost?

It depends on how much power you use and what you want to fix. We give a free consultation and a custom quote. Material prices move with purchase and transport costs, so ask for the current figure.
