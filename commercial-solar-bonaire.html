Questions about commercial solar.

What kinds of sites do you cover?

Large commercial facilities, boats, remote sites and vacation homes.

Can you handle a difficult roof?

Yes. We install on sloped, flat, tile and corrugated sheet roofs, and we can build ground frames and structures.

Can you look after a site I am not at?

Yes. We offer remote monitoring and service for vacation homes. We also set up the monitoring app with you before we leave.

Do you build battery rooms?

Yes. Mario on our crew builds battery rooms.

Who does the work?

Our own crew. We have electricians, a construction hand and a warehouse team, all on Kaya Industria Pariba.

What does a custom system cost?

It depends on consumption and your goal. We give a free consultation and a custom quote. Material prices move with purchase and transport costs, so ask for the current figure.
