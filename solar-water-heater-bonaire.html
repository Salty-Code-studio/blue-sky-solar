Questions about solar water heaters.

How much hot water does a Sunpad give?

The Sunpad has a 150 litre tank that heats to 85°C. It gives you up to 380 litres of water at 40°C.

Does a solar water heater need electricity?

No. The Sunpad needs no electricity.

Does it work on cloudy days?

Yes. It still works on cloudy days.

How much maintenance does it need?

Low maintenance.
